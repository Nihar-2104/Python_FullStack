Q1) List all 6 phases of SDLC in order with one line about each.

Ans:Q1) List all 6 phases of SDLC in order with one line about each.

Ans:

SDLC – Software Development Life Cycle

1. Planning – The project requirements, goals, resources, and schedule are identified.

2. Requirements Analysis – The team collects and analyzes what the users and business need from the software.

3. Design – The software structure, user interface, database, and overall system design are planned.

4. Development – Developers write the code and build the software according to the design.

5. Testing – The software is tested to find and fix bugs and make sure it works as expected.

6. Deployment and Maintenance – The software is released to users and regularly maintained, updated, and improved.

Q2) Write 3 differences between Agile and Waterfall models.

Ans:

1. Agile is an iterative model where development is done in small cycles, while Waterfall follows a fixed step-by-step process.

2. Agile allows changes in requirements during development, while Waterfall usually requires requirements to be finalized before development starts.

3. Agile involves frequent testing and customer feedback, while Waterfall generally performs testing after the development phase is completed.

Q3) Name the 3 main Scrum roles and write one line about the responsibility of each.

Ans:

1. Product Owner – Manages the product requirements and prioritizes the work in the Product Backlog.

2. Scrum Master – Helps the team follow Scrum practices and removes obstacles that affect the team's work.

3. Developers – Design, build, test, and deliver the working product increment during each Sprint.

Q4) What is a Sprint in Scrum? Mention its typical duration.

Ans:

A Sprint is a fixed period in Scrum during which the team works on selected tasks and creates a usable product increment.

Typical Duration: A Sprint usually lasts 1 to 4 weeks, with 2 weeks being very common.

Q5) What do these Jira board columns mean: To Do, In Progress, Done, Backlog?

Ans:

1. To Do – Tasks that are ready to be started but have not been started yet.

2. In Progress – Tasks that the team is currently working on.

3. Done – Tasks that have been completed and meet the required conditions.

4. Backlog – A list of tasks, features, bugs, and requirements that may be worked on in the future.
